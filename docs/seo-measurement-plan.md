# SEO Measurement Plan

The purpose of this plan is to measure whether the technical and content work is creating qualified organic visibility, not merely increasing the number of indexed pages.

## Primary KPI groups

### 1. Indexation health

Track weekly:

- Valid indexed URLs
- Submitted vs indexed sitemap URLs
- Crawled - currently not indexed
- Discovered - currently not indexed
- Duplicate/canonical exclusions
- 404 and soft-404 URLs
- Server errors

### 2. Search visibility

Track monthly by query cluster:

| Cluster | Example search intent | Primary landing surfaces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial | research peptides, laboratory research compounds | `/research-peptides`, `/research-compounds`, `/product/*` |
| Analytical testing | HPLC peptide testing, peptide purity testing, peptide mass spectrometry | `/research/*` |
| Documentation | peptide COA, certificate of analysis, batch verification | `/coa`, `/coa/*`, COA research guide |
| Compound-specific | `[compound] research peptide`, `[compound] research profile` | `/product/*`, `/research/compounds/*` |
| Brand | Transcendent Labs | `/` |

Measure:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- Average position
- Number of ranking queries
- Number of landing pages receiving organic clicks

### 3. Organic engagement

Use analytics to measure:

- Organic sessions
- Product-page visits from research content
- Research-profile to product click-through
- Research-guide to catalog click-through
- COA lookup usage
- COA page to product click-through
- Add-to-cart events originating from organic sessions

### 4. Commercial outcome

Track:

- Organic-assisted orders
- Organic conversion rate
- Revenue from organic traffic
- Product categories with strongest organic conversion
- Queries/pages that produce qualified catalog traffic rather than informational-only visits

## Recommended reporting cadence

### Weekly

- Search Console indexing errors
- Sitemap status
- New pages indexed
- Major ranking anomalies
- Production crawl/availability issues

### Monthly

- Cluster-level impressions/clicks
- Top gaining and declining queries
- Top gaining and declining landing pages
- Research-to-product internal click-through
- Organic conversion performance
- Content opportunities derived from Search Console query data

### Quarterly

- Re-evaluate keyword clusters
- Consolidate cannibalizing pages
- Expand winning topics
- Retire or improve thin pages
- Review backlink growth and referring-domain quality
- Compare branded vs non-branded organic growth

## Baseline period

Use the first 28 days after Search Console verification as the initial technical baseline. Do not judge new research content solely by rankings in its first days; measure crawl, indexation, impressions and query discovery first.

## Content expansion rule

A new article or compound-specific research page should have a defined search intent and internal-link role before publication. Avoid publishing pages solely to increase page count.

## Trust rule

Organic growth is subordinate to evidence quality. Do not improve CTR or keyword coverage with unsupported analytical results, fabricated testimonials, medical claims, dosing instructions or human-use language.
