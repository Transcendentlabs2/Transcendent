# Transcendent Labs SEO Keyword Architecture

## Positioning rule

Primary organic positioning: **research-grade peptides and laboratory research compounds supported by analytical evidence, batch traceability, and research-use-only documentation.**

Content must stay in the laboratory/research context. Avoid human-use intent such as dosing, administration, injections, treatment, bodybuilding, weight-loss protocols, or medical claims.

## Commercial / transactional pages

| URL | Primary intent | Primary keyword | Supporting terms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | Brand + category discovery | research peptides | research-grade peptides, laboratory compounds, Transcendent Labs |
| `/research-peptides` | Commercial category | research peptides | research-grade peptides, HPLC tested peptides, peptides for laboratory research |
| `/research-compounds` | Commercial catalog | research compounds | laboratory research compounds, research reagents |
| `/product/[slug]` | Product-specific commercial | `[compound] research peptide` or `[compound] research compound` | HPLC tested, research grade, laboratory research |

## Internal-linking rules

1. Every product page should link back to its parent commercial category.
2. Commercial category pages should link to relevant educational guides.
3. Every educational guide should link to 2–4 related guides plus `/research-peptides` and/or `/research-compounds`.
4. COA articles should eventually link to `/coa` and lot-level verification pages.
5. Avoid creating near-duplicate pages for singular/plural keyword variants; one authoritative URL should satisfy each intent cluster.

## Content-quality rules

- Explain methods and limitations, not only marketing outcomes.
- Distinguish chromatographic purity from molecular identity.
- Do not call testing independent/third-party unless the evidence identifies the external testing source.
- Do not publish fabricated reviews, laboratory affiliations, purity figures, delivery rates, or test results.
- Prefer lot-specific evidence over generic quality claims.
- Keep all public educational content research-use-only and non-medical.
